1

Email encryption

2

Configure whether users in this environment can enable the encryption of emails they receive from SnapLogic. To enable encryption, users must first set up encryption with their email provider, then upload their public key in their Global user settings. Learn more.

3
Allow users to enable email encryption

When enabled, users who upload a public key get encrypted emails. When disabled, no SnapLogic emails are encrypted, even for users who upload a key.
