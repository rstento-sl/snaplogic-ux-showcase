Cross-cutting issues

Capitalization

JellyRoll specifies sentence case for titles and buttons. APIM 3.0 mixes title and sentence case throughout the UI.

Hidden actions

Buttons across APIM 3.0 hide further actions behind a caret, with nothing to say what they are. Add service hides one, other buttons hide four or five, and when you edit a service or an endpoint they have nothing to do with the button at all.

In-app UA

Some pages flag an in-app user assistance opportunity — a place where in-app UA could work better than static page text. A short guided walkthrough can explain a multi-step or easily-confused setting more clearly than a dense note or a tooltip, without adding permanent clutter to the page.
