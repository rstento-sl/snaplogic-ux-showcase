Expire all passwords?

This immediately signs out every affected user, including you, and forces them to reset their password.

  • You'll receive reset links in individual emails for 3 service accounts. After setting a new password, don't forget to update the dependent app or integration with the new password.
  • Users who log in with SSO are not affected.
  • Users whose accounts are only in environments you don't administer are not affected.
